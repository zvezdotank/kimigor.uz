At work

About

CEO of Wunder Digital Uzbekistan and a digital marketing expert with more than 13 years in the industry: advertising investment efficiency, performance marketing and the adoption of modern digital solutions.

He has led the Wunder Digital office in Uzbekistan since it was founded and is responsible for the company’s strategic development on the local market. Before joining Wunder Digital he held leadership roles at large international companies, responsible for digital promotion. He works with the market’s biggest advertisers: Samsung, Beeline, Pepsi and others.

Listed among the top 35 marketers in Uzbekistan, he represents the digital committee of the Marketing Association of Uzbekistan, speaks at forums on digital transformation, analytics and the future of advertising, and serves on juries of professional awards.
